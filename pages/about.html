North Star Bakery is a family-owned and operated bakery serving our neighbourhood with love for the last 15 years, currently at 2 different locations. We are proud to say we have been nominated for the best bakery in the town for 5 years in a row. Our first location opened in 2011 at Downtown and became one of the top choices of the community and businesses. We have catered to around 60+ events around the GTA. The North Star Bakery is not just a name but a brand that is built on the trust of our customers. We take pride in using only the finest ingredients, making every bite a delightful experience.

Meet our Staff:

Founder

After training under a Michelin chef, John started the bakery in 2011. He started the Downtown location with his signature products and expanded the business to other parts of the city by opening another Bakery @ Steeles location. Today, he oversees all the day-to-day activities and seasonal menus of the bakery. He has a vision to build a nationwide chain of bakeries, and he is actively working on making that vision a reality.

Head Bakers

Monica and Mark are our dough wizards. They manage the bakeries and make sure the doughs are proofed and ready to bake before we start the day. After John, they have also created their own signature items as well, which are very popular too.

Cake Artists

Leo, Jenna, Brian, and Sam are the cake artists who take pride in decorating and customizing cakes as per customer needs. They have a keen eye for detailing, which is reflected in the work they do.

Front of the House

Adam, Eric, Ella, Eve, and Chris are the members of our front of the house. They are the backbone of our bakeries and handle all the daily tasks that keep the bakery moving.
